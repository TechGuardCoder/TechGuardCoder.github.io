NK Data

2026 · installing

Problem

Organizations hold documents with personal and health data they do not know about, and they often keep them past the retention period. Sending those documents to a cloud classifier would expose the very data the scan is meant to protect. Pattern based DLP finds a Social Security number but misses the contextual cases.

What I built

  • One command walks a directory and runs each document through three layers of local models.
  • The first layer finds fixed format identifiers, such as Social Security and card numbers.
  • The second finds names, addresses, medical conditions and dates of birth in free text.
  • The third reads each document in context and decides whether it is sensitive, what class of data it holds, and which retention tier applies.
  • A retention schedule (HIPAA, IRS, FLSA) maps each finding to a tier and flags documents kept too long.
